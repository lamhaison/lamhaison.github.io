## 65. attorney

- Type: Noun (n.)
- IPA (US): /əˈtɝːni/
- English Meaning: A lawyer who is qualified to represent clients.
- Vietnamese Meaning: Luật sư.
- Example: She consulted an attorney before signing the contract.
- Example Translation: Cô ấy đã tham khảo ý kiến luật sư trước khi ký hợp đồng.
