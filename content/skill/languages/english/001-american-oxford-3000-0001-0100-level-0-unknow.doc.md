## Q: **[english - american - oxford - 3000 - level-0 - unknown] Oxford 3000 - 0001 - 0100 - Unknown Words (Level 0)?**

## 15. accompany

- Type: Verb
- CEFR Level: B2
- IPA (US): /əˈkʌmpəni/
- English Meaning: To go somewhere with someone.
- Vietnamese Meaning: Đi cùng; hộ tống.
- Example: I accompanied my friend to the airport.
- Example Translation: Tôi đã đi cùng bạn tôi ra sân bay.

## 53. affair

- Type: Noun
- CEFR Level: B2
- IPA (US): /əˈfer/
- English Meaning: An event or matter that concerns people.
- Vietnamese Meaning: Sự việc; vấn đề.
- Example: I don't want to interfere in their affairs.
- Example Translation: Tôi không muốn can thiệp vào chuyện của họ.

## 73. aid

- CEFR Level: B2

## Practicing
Dưới đây là bài luyện nói 1 phút dựa trên các chủ đề thời sự thực tế, cố gắng dùng tất cả các từ:

accompany, affair, aid, aircraft, airline, ancient

Speaking Topic: International Aid After a Natural Disaster

Recently, I read a news story about a country that was hit by a severe earthquake. Shortly after the disaster, several countries provided aid to support the affected people. Many tons of food and medical supplies were transported by aircraft because roads had been damaged.

Some international airlines also offered free flights for rescue workers and volunteers. In many cases, family members chose to accompany injured relatives to safer areas.

This event also became an important foreign affair issue because governments needed to cooperate with each other. Fortunately, international support arrived quickly.

The earthquake also damaged some ancient buildings and historical monuments, which was a great loss to the country's cultural heritage.

Từ khóa trong bài
* aid = viện trợ
* aircraft = máy bay
* airline = hãng hàng không
* accompany = đi cùng
* foreign affair(s) = vấn đề đối ngoại
* ancient = cổ xưa, cổ đại

Mẫu IELTS Speaking Band 6-7

Recently, I read about a major earthquake. Several countries provided foreign aid, and military aircraft delivered emergency supplies. Some airlines supported rescue missions, and many people accompanied their family members to safe areas. The disaster also damaged several ancient buildings. In addition, it became an important foreign affairs issue because many governments were involved in the relief efforts.
